Wild Run

Raise four baby runners. Feed them, train their powers, and race them across the world.

Sync your runners

  1. On GitHub, open Settings → Developer settings → Fine-grained tokens.
  2. Make a key with access to the wild-run repo and Contents: Read and write. Your NFL key works too if it includes wild-run.
  3. Copy the key (it starts with github_pat_) and paste it here. Do this once on each device.

Who's playing?

How it works: every runner starts as a baby. Keep them fed, watered and happy, train their special power, and run courses to earn XP. They grow from Baby to Young to Grown as they level up.
